Structured documentation, ILS thinking, and learning by building

I have 13+ years of experience in IT support, troubleshooting, identity management, and internal documentation. I am building on that background toward technical documentation and ILS, with a particular focus on S1000D and structured information.

I learn standards by building with them: a CSDB sandbox to understand data-module relationships and workflow, S1000D migration cases to understand modularization and applicability, and an STE checker with regression tests to explore ASD-STE100 in practice. Some projects are production-style case studies; others are purpose-built learning tools designed to make complex standards concrete.

Selected Projects

S1000D Mini-CSDB — Interactive Sandbox

An interactive browser-based CSDB emulator built around the S1000D Issue 6 Bike sample dataset. Explore data modules, publication modules and ICN objects, follow references and where-used relationships, inspect issue history and source XML, and simulate a controlled author > review > approval workflow with a separate authoring-editor view.

HX-20 Legacy Maintenance Manual to S1000D

A realistic S1000D migration case study showing how a fictional helicopter maintenance procedure can be audited, split into data modules, assigned DMCs, structured with applicability for MK1/MK2 variants, and assembled through a publication module. Includes source DOCX, PDF preview, mini DMRL, simplified S1000D-style XML modules, operational test module, and ICN-style technical illustration.

S1000D DMC DMRL Applicability XML Maintenance Documentation ILS

STE Compass — ASD-STE100 Checker

Browser-based authoring aid for Simplified Technical English based on ASD-STE100 Issue 9. Checks procedural and descriptive text, separates deterministic rule findings from author review, supports project-specific technical terminology, and includes regression tests for known false positives.
